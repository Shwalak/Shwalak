## How I work

- **Write the decision rule before the result.** Launch gates, kill criteria and interview thresholds are fixed in advance.
- **Say what is real.** Simulated, unverified and anecdotal are labelled as such, not blended into a confident story.
- **Keep a list of what would change my mind**, and change the plan when the evidence says so.
